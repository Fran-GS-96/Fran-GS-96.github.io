Interactive Resources

Browser-based tools I built for teaching atmospheric dynamics. Each one runs entirely in the page — move the sliders and the fields, diagnostics and diagrams update live. No install, nothing to download.

Built for Dynamics of the Atmosphere · 2026 S2 · Escuela Técnica Aeronáutica in Spanish

  • Standing-wave geopotential

    Geopotencial de onda estacionaria

    The geopotential field of a stationary wave, with its geostrophic wind components and relative vorticity. Sliders for the wave amplitude, wavelength, background zonal wind and the Coriolis parameter — including negative f₀ for the southern hemisphere.

  • Geopotential tendency equation

    Holton, An Introduction to Dynamic Meteorology (4th ed.), §6.3.1, eq. (6.23)

    Relates the local geopotential tendency to two forcings you can read straight off a map: absolute vorticity advection and differential temperature advection.

  • Quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity

    Holton, §6.3.2, eqs. (6.24)–(6.25)

    Shows the geopotential tendency equation for what it really is — a conservation law. Drag a column with its own relative vorticity across the domain and watch the three terms of q trade off against each other.

  • The omega equation

    Holton, §6.4.1, eq. (6.34)

    Diagnoses vertical motion from a single instantaneous field: differential advection of absolute vorticity, and the Laplacian of temperature advection.

  • Phase velocity and group velocity

    Dispersive and non-dispersive waves

    Why crests travel at cₚ = ω/k while the envelope — and the energy — travels at cₔ = dω/dk. Animated wave, dispersion curve and Hovmöller diagram, across five dispersion relations from a spring chain to Rossby waves.
